About the House

Curating a living heritage

MIRĀS GHĀR—The Heritage House—is an emerging civilization-led collection concept founded on Trust, Authenticity and Preservation.

What makes the House different

Each collection begins with a civilization

Rather than starting with a commercial product category, the House begins with a cultural narrative. History, landscapes, craftsmanship and symbolism help inform what a collection becomes.

The goal is to create objects that encourage appreciation and curiosity—thoughtful keepsakes that carry a narrative beyond their immediate use.

That ambition requires transparent historical interpretation, disciplined design, ethical relationships and appropriate technical validation, not merely ornate presentation.

The first aromatic chapter

Egyptian Volume I

Our current focus is an aromatic trio inspired by Khepri, Ra and Atum: morning, noon and nightfall. The sculptural glass vessels and book-form presentation translate this concept into a contemporary collectible design.

These are modern creations inspired by Egyptian themes, not claims of ancient provenance or validated therapeutic effects.

The House's role

Identity

The House maintains its own visual identity, collection narratives and approved product specifications.

Collaboration

Qualified craft and manufacturing partners may execute approved concepts under clear quality and ownership terms.

Care

Growth

Future heritage chapters will be introduced deliberately, with their own research, design vocabulary and development timelines.
