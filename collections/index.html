Royal Presentation Case

A chapter to keep

The book-style case brings together the three 10 ml conceptual aromatic vessels, an illustrated heritage booklet, an oval pelican pendant and a certificate compartment. Seafoam-to-sage leather, warm gold details and carefully arranged cavities form the visual language of this design.

This is an approved design direction, not an offer to purchase. Formulas, physical mechanisms, materials, safety testing and UK regulatory classification remain subject to validation.
