Policies & principles

Clear information matters as much as beautiful presentation. These are our current visitor-facing principles for the prelaunch website.

Prelaunch information · not final sale terms
Publication note: The collection is still under development. Before opening an online store, our full business details, UK-specific consumer terms, return procedures, safety disclosures and applicable privacy documentation must be finalized and reviewed.

1. Product status and authenticity

The aromatic vessels, presentation case and pendant shown on this site are development-stage designs. Images are visual concepts and are not guarantees of final physical specifications. Historical references describe inspiration rather than claiming reproduction of an ancient Egyptian recipe.

2. Product safety and intended use

Materials, formula safety, any required cosmetic or fragrance assessment, labelling, packaging suitability and applicable UK requirements must be established before commercial release. The website does not provide medical advice; descriptive ritual language should not be interpreted as a treatment or health claim.

3. Sourcing and quality

The House intends to assess suppliers for origin information, material suitability, documentation, traceability and consistent quality. References to standards and future testing are development goals unless clearly identified as verified results.

4. Privacy and cookies

This supplied static website template does not include analytics, advertising cookies or an information-submission form. Your hosting provider may process technical information such as server logs. If analytics, sign-ups or contact forms are added, a service-specific privacy notice and any required cookie controls must be published first.

5. Orders, payments, shipping and returns

No checkout is activated on this prelaunch site. Prices, delivery regions, dispatch times, withdrawal rights, returns and refunds are not yet offered or described as final. Commercial terms will be published before any order is accepted, taking account of applicable consumer law.

6. Cultural interpretation and intellectual property

Our design work draws inspiration from historical symbolism and decorative traditions, while seeking to respect their distinct identities. Site artwork and MIRĀS GHĀR brand assets should not be copied or used commercially without the rights holder's permission.

7. Accessibility and corrections

We aim for readable text, keyboard-accessible navigation and clear alternatives to meaningful images. If information needs correcting or if the site is difficult to use, please contact the House once the official channel is published.
