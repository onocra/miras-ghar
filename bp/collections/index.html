Royal presentation case

An heirloom in the making.

The three-panel book-inspired case is designed to hold the three 10 ml vessels, a heritage booklet, an oval pendant with ceramic-disc concept and a certificate compartment. Rich sage upholstery, delicate stitching and warm metallic decoration unite the collection.

Our design approach gives the case equal significance to its contents: something to display, explore and retain.

Illustrated design master; dimensions, closures, materials and safety validation remain under development.

The aromatic pendant

A keepsake for the ritual.

The concept pairs an oval gold-tone pelican pendant with a removable porous ceramic diffusion disc. The intention is to carry an aromatic experience beyond the bottle in a piece inspired by the House emblem.

Engineering, oil-contact suitability, safe-use instructions and diffusion performance must be established through physical prototype review before the mechanism can be described as finalized.

The displayed dimensions, materials and demonstrations are conceptual, not engineering certifications.
