The foundation of MIRĀS GHĀR

Heritage is not simply inherited. It is entrusted.

A heritage house inspired by the knowledge, craftsmanship and stories of civilizations—and the responsibility to present them with care.

The House philosophy

Beyond the object, the story.

MIRĀS GHĀR is conceived not as an ordinary collection of unrelated products, but as a House whose collections are organized around civilizations. Each collection has a cultural identity of its own, joined by the same principles and the belief that craftsmanship and knowledge deserve to remain part of living culture.

“The House does not merely sell heritage—it curates, protects, and respectfully presents it.”

For Egyptian Volume I, that means distinguishing an ancient story from its contemporary interpretation. The collection takes inspiration from historical motifs and the symbolism of Khepri, Ra and Atum. The vessels, scent concepts and collectible book case are modern designs—not claims to reproduce an ancient formula or artifact.

T · A · P

Three enduring principles

T

Trust

Building confidence through transparency, appropriate quality controls and responsible relationships with makers, suppliers and customers.

A

Authenticity

Honouring cultural origin, genuine materials where verified, documented research and clearly identified modern design interpretations.

P

Preservation

Supporting the continued appreciation of traditional knowledge, regional crafts and the stories that give them meaning.

How a collection takes shape

Discover. Research. Refine.

Heritage first, with product quality and technical validation alongside design.

01

Cultural discovery

Research the civilization, its symbols, materials and traditions.

02

Thoughtful curation

Develop distinctive concepts under one coherent cultural narrative.

03

Prototyping & review

Refine vessels, contact materials, presentation and usability.

04

Technical readiness

Assess safety, provenance, documentation and applicable UK requirements before commercial release.
