Meet the Heritage House

One House. Many civilizations.

A long-term vision for collections that unite cultural research, inspired craftsmanship and thoughtful contemporary presentation.

Our reason for being

Curating more than objects.

MIRĀS GHĀR was conceived around the belief that the world's civilizations are remembered through living knowledge as much as monuments. Traditional forms, materials and techniques carry stories worth understanding and sharing.

Each proposed Heritage Collection begins with a civilization rather than a commodity category, and aims to connect a refined object with the context that gives it meaning.

Our beginning

Egyptian Volume I

The first aromatic concept explores the symbolism of morning, noon and nightfall through three sculptural vessels and a collectible book-style case. The history inspires; the modern craftsmanship gives it new form.

“To preserve heritage through authentic curation, exceptional craftsmanship, and meaningful cultural experiences.”
